WW2 ENFIELD
NO.2 REVOLVER

Built in Blender using Boolean cuts to rough out the shape, then pushed into a high poly to nail the hard-surface details. Baked onto the low poly and taken into Substance Painter — aiming for a well-worn look that sits between barely-used and falling apart, balancing the blued steel body against the warm wooden grip.
